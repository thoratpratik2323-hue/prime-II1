# Problem Statement & Challenges - Group 19
**Researcher:** Shweta
**Topic:** Local community space environmental issues
**Core Issue:** Garbage dumping and pollution in local community spaces.

## Stakeholders
- Student volunteers
- Housing societies
- Environmental groups
- Residents who notice these environmental issues

## Challenges & Pain Points
1. Garbage dumping and pollution in community spaces.
2. Lack of transparency in reporting or tracking these issues.
3. Lack of accountability/visibility from responsible entities ("companies can be seen").
4. Low community participation in tackling the problem.

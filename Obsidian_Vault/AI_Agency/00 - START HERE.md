## Before You Read Anything Else

Two things determine whether this works for you, and both happen in week one.

Pick one niche. See [[01c - Pick Your Niche]].

Build one working agent before you pitch anyone. See [[01d - Build One Before You Sell One]].

Everything else in this vault supports those two decisions. People who skip them read every module and never launch.

## Core Build vs Full System

The vault ships in two tiers and the only difference is module 09. Module 09 is the templates layer: the master agent prompt, the intake form, outreach swipe file, sales script, proposal, monthly report, launch checklist, client messages, the service agreement, and the legal skeleton. It is in Full System only.

Core Build is complete on its own. The essential agent prompt is written out in [[03c - Writing The Agent Prompt]] and the pre-launch test checklist in [[03f - Testing Before You Ship]], so you can build and ship without the templates. Anywhere a lesson points at a module 09 file, it also tells you what the file contains so you can build it yourself.

## Two Things To Know About This Vault

**Platform specifics change.** GoHighLevel renames menus and revises pricing regularly. Anywhere you see a VERIFY note, open the platform and confirm the current state before relying on it. The concepts stay true, the click paths do not. See [[02b - GoHighLevel Account Setup]].

**Some things are not mine to answer.** Business formation, contract language, call recording consent, and outreach compliance vary by where you operate and carry real consequences. This vault tells you which questions to ask and flags where the risk is. It does not replace an attorney or an accountant. Module 10 maps where the risks are so that one hour with a professional is productive.

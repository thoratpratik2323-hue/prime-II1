# Build One Before You Sell One

Do not pitch anyone until you have a working voice agent you can call from your phone right now.

This is the single highest leverage rule in this vault, and the one most people skip because building feels harder than talking.

## Why

Your demo is your entire sales process. When a skeptical owner hears an AI answer the phone, handle an interruption, and book an appointment into a real calendar, the conversation stops being about whether this works. See [[04b - The Demo First Method]].

Without a demo you are asking someone to imagine something they have never heard, and pay you for it. That is a much harder sale, and it is the reason most people in this business never close their first client.

## What To Build

Build a complete agent for a fictional business in your target niche. If you picked dental, build "Riverside Family Dental." Give it real hours, a real service list, a real calendar, and a real phone number you can dial.

Make it good enough that if a stranger called it, they would not question it. That standard matters, because that is exactly what your prospect is about to do.

## The Second Reason

Building it teaches you the product. You will discover that the agent talks too long, that it needs explicit instructions to confirm the appointment time back to the caller, that background noise breaks it, that people interrupt constantly.

Every one of those discoveries is something you would otherwise learn in front of a paying client. Learn it now, on a business that does not exist. See [[03f - Testing Before You Ship]].

## How Long This Should Take

Your first agent will take longer than you expect. Somewhere between a full day and a full week depending on how much you fight the platform.

Your tenth will take an hour, because you will have a snapshot and a prompt library. The first one is an investment in every one after it.

Do not let it take a month. Perfect is not the goal. A working agent you can demo beats a beautiful agent that does not exist. Set a hard deadline and ship it.

## After It Works

Call it twenty times. Try to break it. Then record a clean call and turn that recording into your outreach asset. That recording is worth more than any pitch deck you could build. See [[04c - Outreach That Gets Replies]].

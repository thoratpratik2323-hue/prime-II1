# The Minimum Stack

You need five things. Not fifteen. Tool sprawl is how beginners spend three months configuring software and never talk to a prospect.

**1. A platform.** The core. It holds the voice agent, the CRM, the calendar, the phone system, and the client sub-accounts. This vault uses GoHighLevel as the worked example. See [[02b - GoHighLevel Account Setup]]. Cheaper and free alternatives, including a stack you can build a demo on for under ten dollars, are in [[11g - The Budget Stack]].

**2. A phone number and A2P registration.** The agent needs a number to answer. Registration is a compliance step that takes days and blocks your SMS features until it clears, so start it early. See [[02c - Phone Numbers And A2P]].

**3. A calendar the agent can write to.** Usually the GoHighLevel calendar, sometimes synced to the client's Google Calendar. Without this the agent takes messages instead of booking, which is a much weaker product. See [[02e - Calendars And Booking]].

**4. A way to get paid.** Stripe for the setup fee and the recurring monthly. See [[06c - Contracts And Getting Paid]].

**5. A domain and a professional email.** you@youragency.com, not a gmail address. Cheap, fast, and it changes how prospects read your outreach. See [[02f - Your Own Infrastructure]].

That is the whole stack for your first ten clients.

## What You Do Not Need Yet

You do not need a website on day one. Your demo recording sells better than a website. Build the site when you have a client, not before.

You do not need a separate voice AI platform on day one. Running one platform instead of two keeps your costs and your support surface small. Specialty voice providers exist and some are better on latency and voice quality, which is worth testing before you commit rather than after. See [[11h - How To Evaluate A Platform In One Day]].

You do not need a logo, business cards, a brand palette, or an LLC formed before your first conversation. All of that is procrastination wearing a costume.

You do not need paid lead lists. Your first clients come from businesses you can find on a map. See [[04a - Building Your Target List]].

## The Cost Floor

Understand what you are committing to monthly before you start, because your pricing depends on it.

You will pay a GoHighLevel plan fee, plus usage on top: phone minutes, voice AI minutes, SMS segments, and email sends are billed separately from the plan. Usage is the part people forget, and it is the part that eats margin if you price wrong. See [[06b - Protecting Your Margin]].

Write the numbers down in one place. [[02k - The Cost Sheet]] is the line by line version: plan fee, phone number rental, voice minutes, SMS segments, email sends, and what one typical client costs you a month.

Get the current numbers from GoHighLevel directly before you quote anyone. Pricing on this platform changes, and the third party blog posts you will find are frequently out of date. See [[02b - GoHighLevel Account Setup]].

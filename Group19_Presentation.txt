GROUP 19 PRESENTATION SCRIPT / OVERVIEW
======================================

1. INTRODUCTION
- Speaker: Shweta
- Role: Leader and Researcher of Group 19

2. PROBLEM DISCOVERY & BRAINSTORMING
- Initially brainstormed and discussed over 5 different problems before finalizing our focus.
- Key problems identified: Garbage dumping, environmental pollution, waste mismanagement, green space damage, difficulty reporting trash, and lack of monitoring.

3. TARGET AUDIENCE / STAKEHOLDERS
- Student volunteers, housing societies, and environmental groups who notice community issues firsthand.

4. THE FOUR CORE PROBLEMS
- Problem 1: Daily reporting is difficult because there is no simple, dedicated platform.
- Problem 2: There is a lack of transparency in how issues are resolved.
- Problem 3: Tracking accountability across responsible entities/companies is tough.
- Problem 4: There is low community participation in civic maintenance.

5. OUR SOLUTION
- A simple, intuitive platform to report, track, and resolve environmental and civic issues collaboratively, boosting transparency and community engagement.
